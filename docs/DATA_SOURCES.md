# Data sources

Every candidate below must be checked before use (still online, licence, terms of use). Record the result and download date here. Candidates marked "to verify" have not been checked yet.

## Part A: League of Legends

| Dataset | Where | Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Riot Games API (match v5, timeline) | https://developer.riotgames.com | The source used by Dr Hojaji. Needs a free developer key, rate limited. Follow Riot's API terms; do not redistribute raw data. Best for timeline features (gold, objectives, wards, positions per minute). | to verify |
| Oracle's Elixir professional match data | https://oracleselixir.com/tools/downloads | Free yearly CSVs of professional games with team and player stats (gold at 10 and 15 minutes, dragons, towers, wards, vision score). Check the terms on the site. | to verify |
| League of Legends Diamond Ranked Games (first 10 minutes), Kaggle | https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/bobbyscience/league-of-legends-diamond-ranked-games-10-min | About 10,000 high elo games, 10 minute snapshot, blue win label. Small and easy for a first baseline. Check the Kaggle licence. | to verify |

## Part B: rugby union

| Dataset | Where | Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kaggle rugby union datasets | https://www.kaggle.com/search?q=rugby+union | Search for match level team statistics (Six Nations, Premiership, URC, Super Rugby, World Cup). Check licence and how the data was collected. | to verify |
