## Her papers to read and reproduce

1. Deep learning techniques for identifying KPIs in League of Legends (Computers in Human Behavior Reports, 2025): 154,000 games from the Riot API, neural networks, about 97% win prediction. Top KPIs: fewer turrets lost, higher bounty level, more turrets destroyed, less damage taken, more dragons. Higher skilled players stay near objectives with better ward placement and vision control.
2. Understanding Competitive Skill in League of Legends (Frontiers in Sports and Active Living, 2026): over 35,000 ranked games (2024, patches 14.1 to 14.7). Higher ranked teams manage information, communicate, move and position better rather than simply fighting more.
3. AI enabled prediction of sim racing performance using telemetry data (Computers in Human Behavior Reports, 2024): 174 drivers, 1,327 laps, hybrid feature selection (two correlations plus three ML models), 97.19% accuracy; top features average speed, lateral acceleration, steering angle, lane deviation.
4. Optimizing Sim Racing Performance Using Machine Learning and Evolutionary Algorithms (ICAART 2026): KPIs used as the fitness of an evolutionary algorithm to generate ideal laps.
